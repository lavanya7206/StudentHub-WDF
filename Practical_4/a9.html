Frequently Asked Questions

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a platform that provides students with important campus information, events and services.

How can I register?

You can register by visiting the Register page and filling in the required details.

How can I login?

Visit the Login page and enter your registered username and password.

Where can I find campus events?

You can view upcoming campus events on the Events page.

Can I update my profile?

Yes, you can manage and update your information from the Profile page.

How can I give feedback?

You can submit your suggestions and feedback through the Feedback page.
