Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a platform that helps university students access announcements, events, profiles, and other campus information.

2. How can I register?

Go to the Register page, fill in your details, and click the Register button.

3. How do I log in?

Open the Login page, enter your email and password, and click the Login button.

4. Can I update my profile?

Yes. Visit the Profile page and click the Edit Profile button.

5. How can I contact StudentHub?

Visit the Contact page and use the contact form or the provided email and phone number.
